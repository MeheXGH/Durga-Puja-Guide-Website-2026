Complete Transit & Road Guide

Map lines are orientation overlays, not surveyed track geometry. Confirm live metro/rail service, station entrances, platform and Puja diversions before travelling.

1. Train to Dum Dum Jn

Board suburban local train from your station directly into Dum Dum Junction. Follow the underground signage directly toward the Blue Line Metro platforms.

Dum Dum Maps

2. Blue Line Metro → Kalighat Station

Board southbound Blue Line train to Kalighat Metro Station. Exit towards Rashbehari Crossing / Chetla Lock Gate road.

Kalighat Metro Maps

3. Chetla Auto Stand & Pandal Circuit

Walk or take a shared auto from Kalighat Metro Auto Stand over the canal into Chetla. Start at Chetla Agrani and hop eastward towards Gariahat.

4. Return via Ballygunge Jn → Sealdah

Finish your last stop at Ballygunge 21 Pally. Walk 15 mins to Ballygunge Jn Railway Station. Board any Sealdah-bound local train (12–15 min run) and transfer seamlessly to your northbound train home.
